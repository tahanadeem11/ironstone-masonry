Every property is different

Not every project fits a standard service. If you have an idea that involves masonry or outdoor construction, we want to hear about it. As a custom masonry contractor in Brewster, NY, we will visit your property, talk through what you need and give you an honest, no-obligation estimate.

Examples of custom projects

  • Custom stone or brick features and monuments
  • Entry columns, piers and gate surrounds
  • Mixed-material combinations of stone, brick, pavers and concrete
  • Unusual repairs and restorations of older masonry
  • Projects that combine several of our services

How we work on custom projects

  1. Tell us your idea by phone or through our contact form.
  2. On-site consultation to look at the space and discuss options.
  3. Clear estimate with materials and scope explained.
  4. Careful build with direct owner oversight.
