Small upgrades, big difference

The right feature can change how you use your whole property. As an outdoor improvement contractor in Brewster, NY, we add the elements that make outdoor space more comfortable, safer and better looking, built from stone, pavers and concrete that fit your home.

Popular outdoor improvements

  • Fire pits and fireplaces: stone and paver fire features for year-round gatherings.
  • Seating walls: built-in seating that doubles as patio edging.
  • Outdoor kitchens: masonry grill islands and counter bases for cooking outdoors.
  • Steps and entries: safe, attractive steps, landings and entry columns.
  • Lighting and finishing touches: details that make spaces usable after dark.

Plan it as a whole

Many improvements work best when planned together with your patio, walkways and stonework. Tell us what you have in mind and we will help shape it into a practical, well-built plan with a clear estimate.

Built to handle the seasons

Outdoor features face rain, snow and repeated freezing. We build with compacted bases, correct drainage and durable materials, so fire pits, walls and steps stay solid year after year.

FAQs

Outdoor Improvements Questions Answered

What outdoor improvements do you build?

We build fire pits, fireplaces, seating walls, steps, outdoor kitchens and living areas, and add lighting and finishing details.

Can you build an outdoor kitchen?

Yes. We build masonry bases and islands for grills and countertops as part of outdoor living areas.

Can improvements be added to an existing patio?

Often, yes. It is easiest when planned in the original design, but we can add seating walls, fire features and steps to many existing patios.

Do you help with design?

Yes. We discuss your goals, recommend layouts and materials, and give you a clear estimate before work begins.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We offer free, no-obligation estimates throughout Brewster, NY and nearby Connecticut and New York towns.
