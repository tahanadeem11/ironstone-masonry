Retaining walls that do real work

Sloped properties need walls that hold back soil, control erosion and create usable level space. As a retaining wall contractor serving Brewster, NY and nearby towns, we build with drainage, footing depth and proper backfill in mind, so the wall stays straight for decades instead of leaning or cracking.

Wall types we build

  • Natural stone walls: dry-stack or mortared walls with a rugged, natural look.
  • Segmental block walls: engineered interlocking block in many colors and textures.
  • Terraced walls: tiered walls and planting beds that turn steep slopes into usable garden space.

Drainage is what keeps walls standing

Water pressure behind a wall is the leading cause of failure. We build with a compacted stone base, free-draining gravel backfill, filter fabric and a perforated drain pipe where needed. Learn the warning signs in our guide on when to repair, rebuild or fix drainage.

Repairing leaning or cracked retaining walls

If your wall is bulging, leaning, cracked or washing out soil, call us early. Minor issues can sometimes be repaired, while walls with a failed base or no drainage usually need to be rebuilt correctly. Taller walls may also require permits or an engineered design depending on your town, and we can explain what applies.

FAQs

Retaining Walls Questions Answered

Why is my retaining wall leaning or bulging?

Most commonly excess water pressure from poor drainage, a settled base or soil pushing harder than the wall was designed to resist.

Do retaining walls need drainage?

Yes. Even short walls hold back soil that collects water. Gravel backfill and drain pipe let water escape so pressure does not build up.

Do I need a permit for a retaining wall?

Many towns require permits or engineered designs once a wall exceeds a certain height, often around three to four feet, or if it supports a structure or driveway. Requirements vary by municipality.

What is better: natural stone or block?

Both work well when built correctly. Natural stone has a more organic look, while segmental block offers consistent engineered performance and many styles.
