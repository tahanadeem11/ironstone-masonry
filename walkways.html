Walkways that welcome and last

Your walkway is the first thing guests walk on and the first thing they notice. As a walkway contractor in Brewster, NY, we design paths that look right with your home and perform through snow, ice and rain, from straight front-door paths to curving garden walks.

Walkway options

  • Paver walkways: interlocking pavers in many patterns, with borders and inlays.
  • Natural stone walkways: flagstone and bluestone for a classic, textured look.
  • Brick walkways: traditional herringbone and basket-weave patterns.
  • Steps and landings: safe, consistent risers and treads that match the walkway.

Built on a compacted, well-drained base

Settled, uneven or heaved walkways are almost always a base problem. We install walkways on compacted crushed stone with proper drainage, so water moves away and frost heave is minimized. If your existing walkway has sunk or become a trip hazard, we can lift, rebuild the base and re-lay it.

Safety and curb appeal

A good walkway is slip-resistant, evenly graded and well lit. We pay attention to step height, landing size and surface texture. A new walkway is also one of the most visible curb appeal upgrades you can make. See how it pairs with our patios and retaining walls.

FAQs

Walkways Questions Answered

What is the best material for a walkway in New York and Connecticut?

Pavers and natural stone both perform well when installed on a proper base. The best choice depends on your home style, budget and the look you want.

Can you fix a sunken or uneven walkway?

Yes. We lift the stones or pavers, correct the base and drainage, and re-lay the walkway level.

Do you build steps and landings?

Yes. We build steps and landings in stone, brick, paver or concrete to match your walkway and meet safe proportions.

How do you prevent walkways from heaving in winter?

A compacted, free-draining stone base and correct slope keep water from collecting beneath the surface, which reduces frost heave.

Do you provide free walkway estimates?

Yes. We offer free, no-obligation estimates throughout Brewster, NY and nearby Connecticut and New York towns.
